# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary users are senior leaders and executives in a shipbuilding and heavy-industry enterprise. They use the mobile cockpit in short, high-attention moments: before meetings, during management reviews, while traveling, or when they need to quickly understand company-wide operational health.

Their job is to scan the overall situation, identify abnormal signals, compare themes such as operations, finance, production, procurement, quality, and energy, then drill into the right page without losing context. The interface must support fast judgment before detailed analysis.

Secondary users include business managers, digital operations teams, and project stakeholders who use the prototype for review, demonstration, and alignment on mobile dashboard design standards.

## Product Purpose

This product is a mobile digital operations cockpit for COSCO Shipping Heavy Industry. It brings core operating indicators, risk summaries, theme navigation, chart panels, tables, and drill-down pages into one coherent mobile experience.

Success means a senior leader can understand the company's operating condition from the first screen, notice the most urgent risks, and confidently enter a relevant topic page for deeper information. The product should reduce scattered reporting, reduce cognitive load, and make enterprise data feel as usable as a polished consumer app.

## Positioning

A high-level mobile cockpit that turns heavy-industry operating complexity into clear, trustworthy, and quickly actionable executive insight.

## Brand Personality

The brand personality is stable, premium, and approachable.

The interface should feel authoritative enough for a central enterprise and heavy-industry context, but not cold or bureaucratic. It should borrow the lightness, clarity, and touch-friendly confidence of mature consumer apps while preserving enterprise trust, accurate data hierarchy, and operational seriousness.

The mascot and brand assets should add warmth and memorability without weakening the platform's executive credibility.

## Anti-references

This product should not look like a raw backend admin panel, a generic BI template, a scattered Figma prototype, or a decorative technology dashboard with excessive gradients and unreadable data.

Avoid inconsistent card headers, mixed icon styles, heavy gray blocks, hard section cuts, arbitrary shadows, random font sizes, overly busy tables, and page-by-page visual drift. Avoid making the product too playful: C-end polish is welcome, but it must not become a casual lifestyle app.

## Design Principles

1. Executive clarity first: the first screen must reveal company-level operating status and risk priority without requiring exploration.
2. Consumer-app polish, enterprise restraint: use soft surfaces, unified icons, confident spacing, and touch feedback while keeping the tone trustworthy and measured.
3. One visual language across all themes: operations, finance, production, procurement, quality, and energy pages should share the same header, cards, icons, typography, and entry patterns.
4. Data must scan before it explains: numbers, units, trend labels, chart panels, and tables should be aligned around fast recognition and consistent hierarchy.
5. Warmth should support trust: mascot and friendly details are secondary accents, never substitutes for clear data and reliable structure.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA as the project standard. Text contrast, semantic color usage, touch target sizing, reduced-motion behavior, and keyboard/screen-reader basics should be considered part of the product quality bar.

Color must not be the only indicator for risk or status. Important interactive regions should have clear hit areas and visible feedback. Motion should remain subtle and state-driven, with reduced-motion alternatives.
